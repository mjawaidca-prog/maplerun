# Brand & Business

## Name: **MapleRun** (working decision)
"Payroll run" + the maple sap "run" — distinctly Canadian, evocative of the core action of the product, brandable, no known payroll-industry collision (existing Canadian players: Wagepoint, Payworks, Humi, Knit, Rise, Nethris, Dayforce, ADP, QBO Payroll — none adjacent to this name). Tagline candidates: "Payroll that runs itself." / "Canadian payroll, done in minutes."

Alternates (if trademark/domain search fails): NorthPay · BorealPay · TrueNorth Payroll · LooniePay · Polaris Payroll.

**Launch checks (Phase 7, ~1 hr + fees):** CIPO trademark database search → domains `maplerun.ca` (CIRA) + `getmaplerun.com` fallback → social handles → provincial business registration / federal incorporation decision → E&O + cyber insurance quote (payroll SaaS standard).

## Design language (Phase 2 implements)
- Feel: trustworthy fintech, warm not sterile. Deep maple red `#B3261E`-family as accent on a calm neutral base (stone/slate), generous whitespace, large numerals for money.
- Typography: Inter (UI) + tabular figures for amounts. Dark mode from day 1.
- Patterns to copy: QBO's pay-run wizard clarity; Wagepoint's friendliness; ADP's report depth (without the clutter).
- Components: shadcn/ui; charts Recharts; every money figure right-aligned, two decimals, `CA$` only where ambiguous.

## Pricing draft (decide finally at Phase 6)
| Plan | Price (CAD/mo) | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Solo | $15 + $3/employee | 1 company, unlimited runs, stubs, remittance reports |
| Growth | $25 + $3/employee | + T4/ROE, exports, multi-admin, priority support |
| Accountant | $59 + $2/employee | multi-company workspace |
Benchmarks: Wagepoint ≈ $22.50 + $4.50/emp; QBO Payroll ≈ $25–30 + $6/emp; ADP custom-quoted higher. 30-day free trial, no card up front. Year-end (T4) season is the natural upsell moment.

## Target customer & wedge
Canadian SMBs 1–25 employees (restaurants, trades, clinics, agencies) currently on spreadsheets + PDOC, or over-paying ADP. Wedge: cheapest accurate calculator with beautiful stubs and zero learning curve; grow into remittance reminders + year-end automation.
