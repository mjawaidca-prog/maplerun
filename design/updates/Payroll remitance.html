Here's what was built — the MapleRun Remittance Report, fully matching the existing design system:

Four tabbed views:

  1. Deduction Breakdown — Line-by-line CRA components (CPP employee, CPP employer, EI employee, EI employer, federal tax, provincial tax) with rates, employee/employer splits, totals, and percentage bar charts

  2. By Employee — Individual contribution drill-down showing each employee's CPP (ee/er), EI (ee/er), federal tax, provincial tax, and total CRA owing

  3. Remittance History — 8-month history with submission dates, statuses (Submitted / Pending / Late), amounts per component, and CRA reference numbers

  4. Schedule & Rates — Upcoming due dates calendar + 2026 statutory rates reference (CPP 5.95%, CPP2 4.0%, EI 1.66%, employer ×1.4 multiplier)

Key features:

  • Urgent alert banner for the Feb 15 due date
  • Summary cards showing total owing ($15,126.32), CPP, EI, and tax totals
  • Account info strip with business number, remitter type, filing status
  • "Submit to CRA" primary action button
  • It's also added as a 6th tab in the Full Package
